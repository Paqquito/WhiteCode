---
name: checklist
description: Prepare a short evidence-based review checklist for the current change.
invocation: both
tools: []
---
Read the current change and the repository's own contribution instructions. Identify the smallest relevant checks for correctness, permissions, sensitive data and compatibility. Present a short checklist tied to concrete files and risks. Do not invent test results, grant permissions, edit files, commit, publish or send messages. Ask before expanding the requested scope.
